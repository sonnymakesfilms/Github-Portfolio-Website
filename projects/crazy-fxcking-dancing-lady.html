About the project

Upon finalising her divorce, Ellen begins to see a mysterious pink-haired fairy who draws her into a surreal journey of self-discovery, gradually encouraging her to embrace the freedom and joy of a new beginning.

Produced as my graduate project at the University of Bristol, the film was selected to receive university-funded professional colour grading and sound mixing at Films at 59 ahead of its planned festival run in 2027.
