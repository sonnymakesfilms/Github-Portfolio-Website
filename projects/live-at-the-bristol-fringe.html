About the project

Live at the Bristol Fringe captures a live performance by musician Freddie Perfect, recorded using a multi-camera setup.

I co-directed and produced the recording, working alongside the camera team to capture the performance, before editing the footage into a series of live music videos that showcase the energy and atmosphere of the performance.
